TRANG DANG KY DAO TAO LAI XE AN TOAN (Google Sheet + Apps Script + GitHub Pages)
==============================================================================

- Code.gs         : ma chay tren Google (luu nguoi dang ky vao Google Trang tinh)
- ../index.html   : trang dang ky (len mang: https://tpk792023.github.io/dang-ky-lai-xe/)
- ../mau.docx     : file mau danh sach, trang quan ly dung de xuat Word

BUOC 1 - TAO GOOGLE TRANG TINH
1. Vao sheets.google.com -> Trang tinh trong. Dat ten: "DK lai xe an toan Vinh Hau".
   (Khong can tao cot, may tu tao trang tinh "DangKy" khi co nguoi dang ky dau tien.)

BUOC 2 - DAN MA
1. Tien ich mo rong -> Apps Script.
2. Xoa het noi dung co san, dan toan bo file Code.gs vao.
3. Mac dinh KHONG can ma: var MA_QUAN_LY = '';  -> ai mo trang quan ly cung xem duoc danh sach.
   Muon khoa lai: go ma vao giua hai dau nhay, vi du var MA_QUAN_LY = 'vinhhau2026';
4. Bam Luu.

BUOC 3 - TRIEN KHAI
1. Trien khai -> Tuy chon trien khai moi -> banh rang -> Ung dung web.
2. Thuc thi duoi dang: Toi.    Nguoi co quyen truy cap: Bat ky ai.
3. Trien khai -> cap quyen (Nang cao -> Di toi ... -> Cho phep).
4. Sao chep "URL ung dung web" dang https://script.google.com/macros/s/.../exec
   va GUI CHO CLAUDE de gan vao trang (bien API_URL), roi dua len GitHub.

SU DUNG
- Gui link https://tpk792023.github.io/dang-ky-lai-xe/ cho moi nguoi (Zalo, ma QR...).
- Nguoi dang ky dien: Ho ten, ngay sinh, dia chi, so GPLX (hoac "chua co"), SDT.
  Cung ho ten + cung SDT gui lai thi chi sua thong tin cu, khong bi trung.
- Quan ly: mo https://tpk792023.github.io/dang-ky-lai-xe/#quanly (hoac bam "Quan ly danh sach"
  cuoi trang) -> danh sach hien ngay -> xem, xoa, "Tai Word theo mau" (dung mau cua Cong an xa,
  cot Ky ten de trong de ky tay).
- Du lieu goc luon nam trong Google Trang tinh, sua truc tiep o do cung duoc.

SUA CODE.GS SAU NAY
- Trien khai -> Quan ly cac ban trien khai -> but chi -> Phien ban: "Phien ban moi" -> Trien khai.
  Lam vay link /exec giu nguyen, khong phai sua trang.
